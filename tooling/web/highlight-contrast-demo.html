CX bracket de-emphasis — three renderings of the same program

1 · No highlighting

[?def greet ($name::string)
  [?match [$env:var 'USER']
    [case [err $e] [greeting anonymous=true]]
    [else [greeting hello=$name count=3 :ok]]]]

Everything competes equally; structure must be parsed glyph by glyph.

2 · Normal highlighting (brackets at full body contrast)

[?def greet ($name::string)
  [?match [$env:var 'USER']
    [case [err $e] [greeting anonymous=true]]
    [else [greeting hello=$name count=3 :ok]]]]

Tokens are colored, but the bright brackets still shout — ~⅓ of the glyphs pull the eye.

3 · Bracket de-emphasis (punctuation dimmed, tokens lead)

[?def greet ($name::string)
  [?match [$env:var 'USER']
    [case [err $e] [greeting anonymous=true]]
    [else [greeting hello=$name count=3 :ok]]]]

Same gold hue as normal, one shade dimmer (~55% luminance) — recognizably brackets, but heads, bindings, atoms, and values lead. This is the CX design intent (tooling/HIGHLIGHTING.md).

3b · Dimmer variant (for comparison)

[?match [$env:var 'USER'] [case [err $e] :anon] [else $name]]

Two shades dim, same hue — kept for side-by-side judgement.
