## The option the owner accepted, verbatim

"a wave may add to the section its ruling names the exact semantics its fixtures grade — each
sentence carrying a case id, no new vocabulary word, no edit outside that section — and the
integrator merges without a letter, listing the sentences in the merge post; anything else stays a
flag and a letter"

## What it settles

The waves of the v0.18 flow ladder (FW-1) each carried sentences in `flow.md` beyond their status
rows, and each went to the owner as a letter that came back (a). The rule names the class so it no
longer needs a letter: a sentence inside the section the wave's ruling names, stating what a fixture
grades, carrying that fixture's case id, adding no vocabulary word. Everything outside that class —
a sentence in another section, a new word, a sentence no fixture grades — stays a RESULTS.md flag
and a letter.
