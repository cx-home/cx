## NEWB-1 — the newest bugs are scheduled first

The launch order of the bug batches follows recency: an issue filed this week (the QUAL-1 harvest — cx-core-code#13–47, cx-core-data#1–26, cx-private #1762–#1778 and what follows them) is scheduled ahead of an older bug or an older enhancement, because a fresh defect is cheapest to fix while its finder's measurement, its fixture and the code it names are current, and because the harvest is the quality signal QUAL-1 asked for. The exception is the owner's: when the working epic — a round already in flight, a landing order already set, a batch whose issues share one fix — makes the older item the better next step, it goes first and the reason is stated on the board. What was measured when ruled: 133 open under `v0.18`, of which about 85 are component and cx-private issues filed since 2026-10-01 by PROP-1, SPECINV-1, ORDK-1, ERRAT-1, the bug batches and their adversarial readers.

## OVER-2 — tens of dollars past a window's fill are spent when the spend is smaller than what a stop would cost

OVER-1's default stands: every launch is paid from the 5-hour window's allotment, nothing launches past the window's taper, no fix is paid from extra usage. What this direction changes: when the window fills with rounds minutes from READY or mid-run, the integrator does not stop them to save the overspend — the rounds finish their turns into the reset, and the extra usage is read with every meter read and posted with its value on #1591 — because a stop costs each round a resume of about 150k tokens and its in-flight turn (measured 2026-10-02/03), which is more than the tens of dollars the minutes cost. The bound is the owner's word: tens of dollars per window, never a window carried on extra usage, never a launch on it. What was measured when ruled: the window at 82 % with 52 minutes to its reset, four rounds in their premerge-flows or writing RESULTS.md, extra usage $0.00 this account.
