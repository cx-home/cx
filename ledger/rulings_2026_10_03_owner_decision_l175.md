## RSIZE-1 — the round is sized to the window, and the pause is the box's time (L175 = (b))

1. **Size.** A round is sized to what one 5-hour window's allotment finishes in agent work: for a bug batch, three to four issues, never one; six to eight only when the issues share one fix. A round ends its turn at "every commit pushed, the premerge-flow started in the background" before the window fills; the verdict is read at the reset and RESULTS.md with `MERGE-READY` follows — a page, not a context rebuild. This amends L160 (b)'s unit; its reason (one-issue rounds paid the round's fixed cost per issue) stands and is why the floor is three.
2. **One preamble.** The nine launch files (AGENT-PREAMBLE-2026-09-24.md with its addenda, HEAD-STATE.md through HEAD-STATE-5.md, the binding sections of AGENT-STANDING-RULES.md as read at launch, RULES-LEARNED.md) are consolidated into one round preamble of at most 120 lines (/Users/dev2/git-repos/cx/ROUND-PREAMBLE-1003.md, owed to the repository by the next rules-touching branch, AGENTS-1); the head and the mains are one HEAD line in the launch message; the primer is read by its section index, whole only by a round that writes CX for the first time. AGENTS.md and AGENT-STANDING-RULES.md stay the rules; the preamble is the launch's reading of them, not a second authority.
3. **Landings.** READY branches land in unions per gap; a round that moves the Makefile or a selection row unions with a READY sibling so the others regrade once.
4. **Unchanged.** Opus for every premerge-flow round, the adversarial reader before READY, fixture before fix, the premerge-flow whole per round, zero extra usage, the six rounds paused at 00:3xZ finish at their size.

Cost: none in dollars (the plan's allotments; extra usage off); per bug, break-even to ~15 % fewer tokens; more premerge-flows and builds in the idle pause; more landings, so unions; ~30 % more integrator traffic. Expected: ~30–35 closes a day on one account once the mix is bug batches, the week's 24 windows finishing the backlog.
