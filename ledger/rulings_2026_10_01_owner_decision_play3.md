## PLAY-3 — two readings: data and code; a former query example is re-filed by what it is

The playground's readings are the site's two words: **data** (a document, shown and projected) and
**code** (every program — a `[?for]` over a bound `$doc`, a CXPath select, a `[?match]` on document
shape, and everything else). "As appropriate" is a rule, not a bulk move: each example that stood
under the query reading is re-filed by what it is — a program goes to code, with its input document
shown real beside it (PLAY-2's rule on inputs stands: never a stub); a plain document goes to data.
The vocabulary is unchanged and is why there is no third grouping: a query is a program over data
(DOCS-51 §1). The classifying rule and the step that refuses a misfiled example, the picker, the
crumbs, the legacy sections, the site's vocabulary page and every sentence that names three readings
follow in one round (#1741); the integrator reads the playground in a browser before the merge; the
item ticks only on the owner's run — five examples of each of the two readings (DOCS-51 §9).
